# 📜 Server Hierarchy & Operational Protocol

To maintain order and ensure the server culture remains consistent, all staff and members are expected to follow the established chain of command.

---

### The Admin (Supreme Authority)

*The architects of the server's culture and ultimate decision-makers.*

* **Final Authority:** Any decision made by the Bro Level is final and non-negotiable.
* **Policy Veto:** Admin  members hold the power to implement, amend, or strike down any server rule at any time.
* **Staff Oversight:** The Admin retains the right to audit, promote, or demote members of the moderation team.
* **Cultural Vision:** You are the guardians of the "vibe." Your word sets the standard for acceptable behavior in the server.

---

### Head of Mod (Executive Management)

*The bridge between the Bro Level and the front-line staff.*

* **Execution:** Ensure all actions taken by the team align with the directives of the Bro Level.
* **Mediation:** Handle complex disputes and user appeals. If a situation is high-stakes, consult the Bro Level before acting.
* **Team Oversight:** Monitor moderator activity and ensure documentation (logs) is kept up to date.
* **Communication:** Act as the primary point of contact for the Bro Level regarding server health and potential threats.

---

### Moderators (Front-Line Enforcement)

*The daily caretakers of the server.*

* **Consistent Enforcement:** Apply rules fairly to all users. When in doubt regarding a "Bro Level" member, consult the Head of Mod first.
* **Documentation:** All kicks, bans, and time-outs must be logged in the designated staff channel with the reason and relevant evidence.
* **Professionalism:** Maintain a neutral, professional tone. Avoid public arguments; move heated conflicts to private channels immediately.
* **Deference:** Recognize and respect the authority of the Bro Level at all times.

---

### Protocol Guidelines

1. **The Admin Override":** In the event that a Admin member reverses a staff action, the matter is considered closed. Staff should use this as a learning moment to understand shifting community expectations.
2. **Private Resolution:** Disagreements between staff tiers must **never** occur in public channels. Use private staff channels for all debates regarding policy or enforcement.
3. **United Front:** Once a policy or decision is finalized by the Bro Level, the entire staff team is expected to uphold it without public pushback.
