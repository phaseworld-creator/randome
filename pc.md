# Laptop Comparison: Dell E7270 vs HP 840 G2 vs HP 745 G6

## 📄 At a Glance Summary

| Feature | Dell Latitude E7270(voby) | HP EliteBook 840 G2(erto) | HP EliteBook 745 G6(phase) | Clear Winner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Release Era** | ~2016 | ~2015 | ~2019 | **HP 745 G6** (4 years newer than G2) |
| **Processor (CPU)** | Intel 6th Gen Core *(Dual-Core)* | Intel 5th Gen Core *(Dual-Core)* | AMD Ryzen 3000 PRO *(Quad-Core)* | **HP 745 G6** (2x cores, modern speed) |
| **Graphics (GPU)** | Intel HD Graphics 520 | Intel HD Graphics 5500 | AMD Radeon Vega 8 / 10 | **HP 745 G6** (Vastly superior) |

---

## 🔹 1. HP EliteBook 745 G6 (Best Overall Choice)

* **Pros:**
  * **Modern Quad-Core CPU:** Modern AMD Ryzen processors handle modern multi-tasking with ease.
  * **Windows 11 Ready:** Meets official Microsoft specs for native Windows 11 updates and security.
  * **Strong Integrated Graphics:** AMD Radeon Vega graphics easily beat older Intel iGPUs for video playback and light work.
  * **Modern Connectivity:** Includes USB-C and sleek slim-bezel housing.
* **Cons:**
  * Generally costs slightly more on the second-hand market compared to older models.

---

## 🔹 2. Dell Latitude E7270 (Runner-Up / Compact Option)

* **Pros:**
  * **Ultra-Portable:** 12.5-inch compact form factor makes it easy to carry in small bags.
  * **Durable Build:** Sturdy, rubberized soft-touch chassis.
  * Slightly newer and faster CPU than the EliteBook 840 G2.
* **Cons:**
  * **No Official Windows 11 Support:** Older 6th Gen Intel chip.
  * Dual-core CPU shows its age with heavy browser tabs or multitasking.
  * Small display can feel cramped.

---

## 🔹 3. HP EliteBook 840 G2 (Oldest & Weakest)

* **Pros:**
  * 14-inch screen gives decent workspace compared to the 12-inch Dell.
  * Extremely cheap on the used market.
* **Cons:**
  * **Outdated Architecture:** 5th Gen Intel Broadwell dual-core CPU is noticeably slow by modern standards.
  * **No Official Windows 11 Support.**
  * Dated design with thick bezels and older port selection (VGA/DisplayPort, no USB-C).

---

## 🏁 Final Ranking

1. **🥇 HP EliteBook 745 G6:** The clear best choice. It is the only modern laptop here, features a 4-core processor, USB-C, and official Windows 11 support.
2. **🥈 Dell Latitude E7270:** Decent budget choice *only* if you specifically want a compact 12.5-inch laptop for light typing and travel.
3. **🥉 HP EliteBook 840 G2:** Hardest to recommend. It is significantly older than the 745 G6 and slower than the Dell E7270.
